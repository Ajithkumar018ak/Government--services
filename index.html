FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How Can We Help You?

Find answers to common questions about using CivicConnect and exploring government services.

What is CivicConnect?
CivicConnect is an information platform that helps citizens discover government services, public schemes, service guidance and relevant official resources.
Can I apply for government services through this website?
CivicConnect provides information and links to official service portals. Actual applications must be completed through the relevant authorised government website.
How can I find a particular government service?
You can use the search option on the homepage or browse the services section to discover relevant service information.
Where can I find government scheme information?
Visit the Government Schemes section to explore scheme categories and find links to relevant official information sources.
Is CivicConnect an official government website?
CivicConnect is an independent information portal concept and does not claim to represent a government department or authority. Please use official government websites for verified services.
How can I contact CivicConnect?
Visit the Contact page to find the available support information and submit an enquiry.
